Privacy Policy — AapdaAi

Last updated: 20 August 2026

AapdaAi is a disaster-response coordination service. People affected by a disaster send reports over WhatsApp; the service organises those reports so that the responsible authorities can see where help is needed and in what quantity.

AapdaAi does not dispatch emergency services. It provides information to the authorities who do. In an emergency, contact your local emergency number directly.

What we collect

Only what you send us, plus what WhatsApp attaches to it:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number — so we can ask follow-up questions and tell you when help has been arranged.
  • The content of your messages — text, voice notes, images and videos you choose to send.
  • Location — only if you share it. Without a location a report cannot be acted on.
  • Message metadata — the time a message was sent and the identifier WhatsApp assigns it, used to avoid processing the same message twice.

We do not collect anything from your device beyond the message you send. No contacts, no browsing activity, no background location.

Why we use it

  • To work out where people need help, how many are affected, and what supplies are required.
  • To group multiple reports about the same event, so ten reports of one fire are not treated as ten separate emergencies.
  • To pass that information to the relevant disaster management authority.
  • To follow up with you and confirm whether help actually arrived.

Who can see it

Reports are visible to authorised disaster-management operators using the AapdaAi dashboard, and to the authority responsible for the affected area.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with anyone outside disaster response.

Automated processing

We use automated language processing to read your message and extract structured details — how many people, whether anyone is injured, what is missing. This helps us respond faster.

No automated system decides whether help is sent. A named human authority reviews and confirms every report before resources are committed.

How long we keep it

Reports are retained for the duration of the disaster response and afterwards for review, so that mistakes in coordination can be identified and corrected. Records that are no longer needed are deleted.

Your choices

  • Sharing your location is optional, though a report without one cannot be dispatched to.
  • You can ask us to delete your reports and your number by writing to the address below.
  • Stop messaging the service at any time and we will not contact you further about that report.

Security

Messages travel over WhatsApp's encrypted transport. Data is stored on servers we control, with access limited to the operators who need it. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

Children

The service is not directed at children. Anyone in danger should send a report regardless of age; we simply do not seek information about a reporter's age or identity beyond their phone number.
